Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

The short version: Deuce is a play-money game. You can play without an account, and then nothing leaves your device. If you make an account, I store your email, your table name and your game progress so you can play on any device and show up on the friends leaderboard. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data, and you can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

Play money only

Deuce Card Room is a simulated casino for entertainment. Chips have no cash value, can’t be bought, won or exchanged for money or prizes, and the app never links to real-money gambling. It’s intended for adults 18 and older.

Without an account

Your bank, VIP level, achievements, records and settings are saved in your browser’s storage on your device. They aren’t sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data or removing the app deletes them.

With an account

When you create an account, these are stored with Supabase, the service that runs Deuce’s database and sign-in:

  • Your email and password. Used only to sign you in. Supabase stores the password in a protected (hashed) form; I can’t see it.
  • Your table name, which you choose.
  • Your game progress: bank, VIP level, achievements, records and app settings, so you can pick up on another device.
  • Whether your account has Deuce Pro in the iPhone app, so your unlocks follow you to any device.
  • Your player card: your table name, bank, level, number of achievements and recent big moments (like “hit the bad beat jackpot”). This is shown to other signed-in players on the leaderboard and brag wall.

The live friends table

When you sit down at a live Hold’em table, your table name, your actions and the cards dealt are shared in real time with the other people at that table through Supabase Realtime. This isn’t stored after you leave.

Services the app loads

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app, and Google Fonts and jsDelivr serve its fonts and code. Like any website host, they receive your IP address and basic browser information when the app loads.
  • Deuce uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and never sells or shares your information.

Deleting your data

Signed in, go to Lounge → Friends → Your account → Delete account. That permanently deletes your account, your cloud save and your player card right away. Progress saved on your device stays there until you clear it.

Children

Deuce isn’t meant for anyone under 18, and I don’t knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has made an account, contact me and I’ll delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/Cody-Ayers/deuce-card-room/issues.
